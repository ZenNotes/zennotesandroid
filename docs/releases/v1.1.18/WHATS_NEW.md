Home Screen widgets.

• New Note: one tap starts a note in your Inbox
• Recent Notes: pinned notes first, then the ones you edited last, with a + to start one
• Today's Tasks: due today, overdue, or undated, overdue first; tap a task to land on its line
• The widgets wear your theme and update within seconds of a change

No new permissions. The widgets read a small on-device summary, never your note bodies.
